Services

Choose what you need, and add more over time

Partnerships start with management support and reporting. Revenue cycle, staffing and brand services are phased in when you decide.

Foundation

Management support, weekly analytics and reporting, financial planning, systems support, payer analysis, marketing analytics, a compliance calendar and growth planning.

Revenue cycle & vendors

Denial and A/R follow-up, write-off approvals routed to you, and vendor management. Billing stays in your name and NPI.

Staffing

Non-clinical staff can be employed and supported by Aevolix and leased back to your practice. Clinical hiring stays with your licensed professionals.

Brand, equipment & space

Support with brand, equipment and space as you renovate, expand or add locations. Your practice stays responsible for its clinical claims.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Do I have to sell my practice?

No. Aevolix partners through a management services agreement. You keep ownership of your practice and its name.

Will you change how we treat patients?

No. Clinical decisions, coding and documentation stay with your licensed professionals. Aevolix supports the non-clinical side only.

Do we have to change our EMR or billing system?

Usually not. We build reporting and workflows around the systems you already use. Claims continue to go out in your practice’s name and NPI.

Which practices are a good fit?

Independent physical therapy and multi-specialty rehab practices in New Jersey, including those offering chiropractic, acupuncture, pelvic floor and neurological rehab, whose owners want to grow without giving up control.

How is Aevolix paid?

Through a management fee set out in the agreement and structured to comply with healthcare fee-splitting and anti-kickback rules. We’ll walk you through it in our first conversation.
