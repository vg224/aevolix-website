Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how Aevolix Health Partners LLC (“Aevolix,” “we”) handles information collected through aevolix.com (the “Website”).

Patients: this Website is not used to provide care or to collect health information. Each practice we support has its own Notice of Privacy Practices, which explains how that practice uses and protects your health information. Where Aevolix handles protected health information for a practice, we do so as the practice’s business associate under a Business Associate Agreement.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional), the type of inquiry and your message. Submissions are processed by Formspree and delivered to us by email.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider receives standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

The Website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. Fonts are served from our own site.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, to discuss potential partnerships, and to keep ordinary business records. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use Website inquiries for advertising profiles.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Website (such as hosting and form processing), when required by law, or as part of a business transfer that keeps these protections in place.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiries as long as needed to respond and for our business records. Depending on where you live, including New Jersey, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Email vg@aevolix.com to make a request.

Children

The Website is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version here. Questions: vg@aevolix.com.

A New Jersey management services organization for independent rehabilitation practices. Aevolix provides non-clinical services. All clinical decisions are made by each practice’s licensed professionals.
