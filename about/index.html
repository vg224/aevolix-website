About Aevolix

Helping independent practices thrive

Aevolix Health Partners is a New Jersey management services organization (MSO). We provide non-clinical services (marketing, technology, revenue cycle support and operations) to independent rehabilitation practices under management services agreements.

Why we exist

Independent practices deliver some of the most personal care in healthcare, but they compete with large networks that have teams for data, billing, marketing and technology. Too often, the only way to get that support is to sell.

Aevolix offers another path. We bring network-grade systems and people to independent practices through a management partnership. Owners stay owners, and clinicians stay in charge of care.

Our team

Our team brings together experience in healthcare operations, engineering, cybersecurity and process automation. We work alongside each practice’s owners and staff every week, so the business side keeps pace with patient care.

Our principles

  • The practice decides. We recommend; owners and licensed professionals decide.
  • Transparency. Weekly numbers, written approvals and a full audit trail.
  • Compliance by design. HIPAA safeguards, Business Associate Agreements, and respect for fee-splitting, anti-kickback and advertising rules.
  • Patients first. Patient-facing work stays in each practice’s own brand and voice.
Referral & industry partners

One accountable partner across our clinics

We work with physicians, health systems, payers, community organizations and vendors to make care easier to reach and simpler to coordinate.

  • Physicians & health systems: multi-specialty rehab (PT, chiropractic, acupuncture, pelvic floor, neuro PT) with clear communication back to you
  • Payers: a single point of contact for contracting conversations, with decisions made by each practice
  • Community partners: education programs such as balance and fall-prevention talks
  • Vendors: consolidated purchasing and evaluation across clinics

We never offer anything of value in exchange for referrals.
