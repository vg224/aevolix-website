What we do

The non-clinical side of your practice, handled

Every service is provided under a management services agreement and phased in when you’re ready.

Analytics & weekly reviews

Weekly dashboards for visits, new patients, cancellations and collections, plus a standing review meeting to act on them.

Financial planning

Budgets, P&L tracking, payer analysis and growth planning, so every decision starts from real numbers.

Revenue cycle support

Denial follow-up, A/R aging and payer-mix review. Claims always go out in your practice’s name and NPI.

Marketing & patient growth

Patient reactivation, Google Business Profile, local search and community programs, all compliant with board advertising and texting rules.

Technology & systems

EMR reporting, Microsoft 365, secure email and texting, websites and online booking, run on a HIPAA-ready platform.

Staffing & HR support

Recruiting, onboarding and HR administration for non-clinical staff, with leased-staff options when you’re ready.

Compliance

A compliance calendar, HIPAA safeguards and Business Associate Agreements, plus help with policies and training.

Vendors, brand & space

Vendor contracts, equipment, space planning and brand support as your practice grows or adds locations.

A different kind of partner

Big-network support without selling your practice

Large physical therapy networks often grow by buying practices. Aevolix works differently: we partner through a management services agreement. You keep ownership of your practice and your name, and licensed professionals keep every clinical decision. We bring the systems, data and people a larger network would provide.

  • Budgets, fees, payer contracts and major vendor decisions stay with the practice’s owners
  • Clinical care, coding and clinical hiring stay with licensed professionals
  • Every recommendation and approval is recorded, so roles are always clear
How partnership works
