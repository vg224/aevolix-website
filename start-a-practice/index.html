Launch roadmap

From plan to first patient

1. Plan

Market and site analysis, a financial model and a launch budget, and service-line planning for your community.

2. Set up

Coordination with your attorney and accountant on entity formation, and help with payer enrollment and credentialing.

3. Build

Lease and build-out guidance, equipment planning, and front-desk and scheduling workflows.

4. Equip

EMR, Microsoft 365, phones, a website with online booking, secure texting and email, ready on day one.

5. Launch

Google Business Profile, local search, physician outreach materials and opening marketing, all compliant with board rules.

6. Run & grow

Billing support, weekly KPIs, staffing help and growth planning, so the business keeps pace with your caseload.

FAQ

Common questions

Who owns the new practice?

You do. Aevolix provides management services under an agreement and does not own or control your clinical practice.

Do you provide legal or accounting advice?

No. We coordinate closely with your own attorney and accountant, who advise you on formation, contracts and taxes.

How long does it take to open?

It depends on location, build-out and payer enrollment timelines. We’ll build a realistic timeline with you during planning.

Can I add chiropractic, acupuncture or other services later?

Yes. Our platform and processes are built for multi-specialty practices, and we can help you plan new service lines when the time is right.
